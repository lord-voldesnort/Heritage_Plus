## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the standard of an institutional, sovereign digital service dedicated to cultural heritage preservation, spatial observation triage, and statutory regulatory compliance. It balances civic authority with state-of-the-art spatial precision. The visual presence is sober, unambiguous, objective, and immutably reliable. 

The aesthetic is Modern Institutional: high contrast, light-dominant utility, crisp delineation, and zero unnecessary visual ornament. It prioritizes data legibility, geospatial telemetry clarity, and evidentiary integrity over whimsical decoration. The UI projects the authority of a national archaeological survey directorate while maintaining the swift usability demanded by field inspectors logging observations on mobile devices under harsh sunlight.

### Target Audience & Core Personas
1. **Field Rangers & Inspectors:** Require high-contrast, glare-resilient UI with tactile, clear touch targets, instant GPS telemetry validation, and foolproof form steps under field conditions.
2. **Conservation Curators & Reviewers:** Require high-density desktop dashboards, dual-pane spatial vector maps, side-by-side comparative photo analysis, cryptographic audit traces, and legally bounded regulatory verdict controls.
3. **Institutional Administrators & Auditors:** Require tamper-evident audit ledgers, compliance verification seals, and print-ready formal regulatory dossiers (A4 aspect standards).

### Emotional Response
Users must feel complete operational confidence, legal assurance, transparency, and accountability. Every distance calculation, buffer classification, and case transition is perceived as objective, tamper-evident, and institutionally vetted.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy & Role Division
The color system operates on an institutional light canvas (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`), establishing an open, clean, and official government administrative standard. Contrast ratios rigorously comply with WCAG 2.1 AAA for text and essential graphical indicators.

- **Primary Brand Color (`#ea580c`):** Strategic institutional warm orange. Used deliberately for primary workflow transitions, key call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, and high-priority system confirmations. It asserts presence without inducing alarm.
- **Secondary Accent Color (`#0284c7`):** Precision sky blue. Assigned to spatial telemetry, geodesic distance vectors, map controls, interactive metadata links, and data-density accents.
- **Tertiary Neutral Anchor (`#0f172a`):** Deep slate navy. Forms the authoritative backbone for primary titles, structural container framing, and data values.
- **Neutral Scale (`#475569` to `#f8fafc`):** Governs structural borders (`#e2e8f0`), secondary labels (`#475569`), subdued text (`#94a3b8`), and background cards.

### Statutory Regulatory Zone Tokens
Statutory zones are hard-coded to universally recognizable, legally calibrated warning and status colors:
- **Core Protected Zone (0–100m) (`#dc2626` / Rose-Crimson):** Indicates immediate statutory violation, strictly restricted development, and critical urgency. Always accompanied by a shield or alert icon and explicit text.
- **Regulated Buffer Zone (100–300m) (`#d97706` / Amber):** Highlights regulated buffer activity, requiring special conservation permissions and secondary inspection. Accompanied by a caution triangle icon.
- **Survey / Compliant Zone (>300m) (`#059669` / Emerald Green):** Confirms baseline compliance, statutory clearances, and strong, high-precision GPS satellite fixes.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy & Font Strategy
The typographic standard leverages `Inter` across all structural levels, establishing an objective, highly legible, institutional environment. Inter's tall x-height and rational letterforms ensure effortless reading across dense case dossiers, complex geospatial metric rows, and low-light mobile field forms.

For coordinates, statutory case identifiers (e.g. `CASE-2026-0841`), cryptographic audit hashes, and raw GPS readings, monospaced typography (`code-telemetry`, `code-dossier`) provides strict vertical alignment and tamper-evident tabular scanning.

### Letter-Spacing & Text Rhythms
- Headings utilize subtle negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) to project crispness and cohesion.
- Category micro-labels, zone pills, and status chips apply uppercase styling with generous letter spacing (`0.06em`) for immediate peripheral recognition by field personnel.
- Body paragraphs preserve standard proportional tracking with comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.625`) to prevent fatigue during multi-page curator reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
This design system utilizes an 8-point baseline grid with a fluid 12-column responsive layout for administrative dashboards, and a single centered column (`max-width: 48rem`) for field capture intake forms to eliminate visual distraction on mobile devices.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack. Field capture controls occupy 100% viewport width with sticky bottom confirmation bars. Margins: `1rem`. Gutters: `1rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column to 12-column adaptive flow. Maps collapse to top halves with bottom scrollable review feeds. Margins: `1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column asymmetric split views. Map inspection workspaces occupy 58–60% width, while metadata and curator decision decks occupy 40–42% width. Margins: `2rem`. Max container bound: `80rem` (`1280px`).
- **Official Print Dossier (`/packet/:caseId`):** Fixed A4 standard layout (`210mm x 297mm`) bounded at `52rem`, with `@media print` rules stripping application navbars, docks, and actions.

## Elevation & Depth

### Elevation Philosophy: Institutional Surface Framing
Depth is communicated through crisp, physical 1px borders and layered surface tiers, rather than dramatic dropped shadows. This ensures that the interface remains readable in outdoor sunlight and renders reliably on legacy institutional monitors and printed compliance dossiers.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#f8fafc`):** The foundational viewport layer. Provides a soft, cool neutral backing that reduces eye strain compared to harsh `#ffffff`.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#ffffff`):** Primary cards, data modules, inspector input cards, and vector map frames. Surrounded by a 1px solid border of `#e2e8f0`.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#f1f5f9`):** Subdued wells, read-only telemetry displays, table headers, and inactive tab containers. Bordered by `#cbd5e1`.
- **Modal / Flyout Overlays (`#ffffff`):** Layered above with an ultra-subtle, clean ambient shadow (`0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) and a structured 1px border (`#cbd5e1`).

### Focus & Active Depth
Interactive elements under focus receive a clean, sharp 2px outline in primary orange (`#ea580c`) offset by 2px white space, guaranteeing accessibility compliance without modal obscurity.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Roundedness
The system implements a controlled **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. Base components use `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radiuses, with container cards scaling to `0.5rem` (`8px`) (`rounded-lg`) and structured modal frames to `0.75rem` (`12px`) (`rounded-xl`).

This subdued rounding preserves the serious, formal geometry suitable for legal and statutory documents while avoiding the abrasive harshness of completely square edges or the informal consumer-app appearance of heavy pill-shaped containers. 

Interactive state badges and numeric counters utilize compact, balanced pill profiles (`px-2.5 py-0.5 rounded-full`) solely to distinguish categorical metadata from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Solid `#ea580c` background, white text (`#ffffff`), `0.25rem` radius, semibold font (`Inter 600`), 1px solid border of `#c2410c`. Hover state transitions to `#c2410c` with subtle shadow (`0 2px 4px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2)`). Height: `44px` on desktop, `48px` on mobile for field touch accuracy.
- **Secondary Action Button:** Surface `#ffffff` background, text `#0f172a`, 1px solid `#cbd5e1` border. Hover state transitions to `#f8fafc` and `#94a3b8` border.
- **Danger / Escalation Button:** Solid `#dc2626` background, white text, 1px `#b91c1c` border. Used exclusively for enforcement escalation and core violation citations.

### 2. Status Chips & Statutory Zone Badges
- **General Architecture:** Compact, inline-flex containers with centered SVG micro-icons, uppercase tracking (`0.06em`), and high-contrast dual-tone fills.
- **Core Zone Badge:** Red bg tint (`#fef2f2`), 1px solid `#fecaca`, text `#dc2626`, with Shield-Alert icon.
- **Regulated Buffer Badge:** Amber bg tint (`#fffbeb`), 1px solid `#fde68a`, text `#d97706`, with Triangle-Caution icon.
- **Survey Compliant Badge:** Emerald bg tint (`#ecfdf5`), 1px solid `#a7f3d0`, text `#059669`, with Shield-Checkmark icon.

### 3. Form Inputs & Textareas
- **Input Fields:** White background (`#ffffff`), 1px solid `#cbd5e1` border, `0.25rem` border radius, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Text: `#0f172a`. Placeholder: `#94a3b8`. Focus state activates a 1px border `#ea580c` with a 2px outer ring in `#ea580c/20`.
- **Field Telemetry Readouts:** Read-only inputs rendered in `#f1f5f9` with monospaced font, right-aligned telemetry badges, and 1px `#e2e8f0` dividers.
- **Prohibited / Banned Terminology Advisory Banner:** Instant validation alert displayed beneath field observation textareas when legally hazardous terms (e.g., "criminal", "illegal encroachment") are detected, offering instant statutory replacements ("alleged unauthorized structure").

### 4. Cards & Containers
- **Data & Metric Cards:** Pure white surface (`#ffffff`), 1px solid `#e2e8f0` perimeter, 1rem to 1.5rem padding. Header rows separate title and category badges with subtle 1px border dividers.
- **Photo Evidence Well (`PhotoDropzone`):** 2px dashed border in `#cbd5e1` over `#f8fafc`. Hovering transitions border to `#ea580c` and background to `#fff7ed`. Includes drag-and-drop triggers and simulated EXIF geotag badges.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Statutory Attestation Checkboxes:** 18px x 18px square box with 3px border radius. Inactive: 1.5px solid `#94a3b8`. Active: Solid `#ea580c` fill with crisp white verification tick. Always accompanied by formal legal attestation copy.
- **Triage Action Radio Deck:** Full-width selectable tile options with 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`), transitioning to 1.5px solid `#ea580c` and `#fff7ed` background when selected.

### 6. Specialized Heritage Components
- **GPS Accuracy HUD:** Sticky top telemetry bar featuring satellite icon, locked status text, accuracy radius in meters (`3.2m accuracy`), and a 4-segment emerald signal strength meter.
- **Immutable Ledger Timeline:** Vertical 2px line in `#e2e8f0` linking sequential case audit nodes. Completed nodes show solid slate icons; active triage nodes show glowing orange beacons; statutory determinations display cryptographic SHA-256 preview hashes in monospaced font.
- **Official Print Dossier (`/packet/:caseId`):** Official institutional layout featuring national emblem placement, formal reference alphanumeric codes, spatial calculation breakdown tables, and curator digital signature seals.
