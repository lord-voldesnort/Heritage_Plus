AMASR Act Statutory Mandate & Legal Text

Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2010:

Section 20A — Prohibited Areas:

Every area, beginning at the limit of the protected area and extending to a distance of one hundred meters in all directions shall be a prohibited area in respect of such monument.

"No person other than an archaeological officer shall carry out any construction in any prohibited area."

Section 20B — Regulated Areas:

Every area extending to a distance of two hundred meters beyond two hundred meters (total 300m) shall be a regulated area in respect of such monument.

Section 20F & Penal Provisions:

Any person who undertakes construction in a prohibited area is liable for rigorous imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years and fine up to one lakh rupees.
