## 2. Script & Action Cue Sheet

### Scene 1: The Field Problem (0:00 – 0:30)
* **Screen**: Site Context Card for *Shivneri Fort* (`/site`).
* **Speaker (Ameya)**:
  > *"Respected Judges, India’s protected heritage faces visible threats every day—unauthorized construction, damage, dumping, and alterations. When a visitor or resident notices a change, they take a photo and send it on WhatsApp. But that report lacks verifiable location, source provenance, boundary context, and review history. Important concerns get lost, or innocent citizens face unfair allegations."*

### Scene 2: Mobile Field Capture (0:30 – 1:10)
* **Screen**: Mobile Field Capture Studio (`/capture`).
* **Action**: Select Category *"Possible Construction"*, show GPS Accuracy HUD ($\pm 4.5\text{m}$), enter neutral description, tap Submit.
* **Speaker (Vivek)**:
  > *"Heritage Pulse solves this. A student or volunteer records an objective observation in seconds. Notice our interface never asks 'Who did this?' and never accuses anyone. It captures raw GPS accuracy, hardware timestamp, and photo context without making legal claims."*

### Scene 3: Spatial Reasoning Engine (1:10 – 1:45)
* **Screen**: Spatial Result View (`/result/HP-MH-2026-0001`).
* **Action**: Pan across Shivneri Fort's source-labelled boundary.
* **Speaker (Vishwajeet)**:
  > *"Our geospatial engine evaluates the observation against the official source-documented boundary using geodesic math rather than fake circular pins. It measures exact boundary distance and tests whether the device GPS uncertainty disk overlaps the perimeter."*

### Scene 4: The Trust Moment (1:45 – 2:15)
* **Screen**: Load Scenario 3 (Near Boundary Edge).
* **Action**: Show boundary distance ($6.0\text{m}$) vs GPS accuracy ($\pm 12.5\text{m}$). The verdict dynamically updates to **Location Uncertain**.
* **Speaker (Vishwajeet)**:
  > *"Here is our core innovation and trust feature: because the GPS error margin overlaps the boundary line, Heritage Pulse refuses to issue a false finding. It flags the case as 'Location Uncertain' and requests additional field evidence. We earn institutional trust by refusing to overclaim."*

### Scene 5: Change Ledger & Reviewer Packet (2:15 – 3:00)
* **Screen**: Change Ledger (`/ledger`) and Reviewer Console (`/reviewer`).
* **Action**: Click *"Export Reviewer Packet"* (`/packet/HP-MH-2026-0001`). Display the generated print-ready dossier.
* **Speaker (Ameya)**:
  > *"Every observation, calculation, and reviewer note is preserved in an append-only Change Ledger. The system produces an audit-ready Reviewer Packet for heritage authorities. Heritage Pulse does not replace the authority; it gives them trustworthy evidence before irreversible cultural loss occurs. Thank you."*
